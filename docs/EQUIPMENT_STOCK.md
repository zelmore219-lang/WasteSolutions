# Equipment stock and physical reconciliation

Equipment stock records known yards, truck stock, customer deployments, maintenance/retirement and loaded disposal holds. Legacy yard stock remains explicitly unspecified until physically reconciled. Creating a yard does not invent equipment movements or historical locations.

Owners, managers and dispatchers record empty-equipment relocation, cart/commercial placement and recovery. Each write requires the current equipment version, the number physically observed on the equipment and a reason. A shared scheduling lock serializes inventory changes with rental reservations/completion. An active destination yard or available carrying truck is required. Maintenance/retired equipment can move between yards while retaining quarantine; it cannot become available truck stock. Only owners/managers retire or reactivate assets. Reservations must be reviewed/released before availability changes. Occupied yards cannot be deactivated.

Cart/commercial placement links an active matching account/address. An optional linked order must already be completed for that account/address and, when equipment came from a truck, match its carrier. Recovery closes the active deployment once and returns it to a known yard or truck. These operations do not create charges, service orders or invoices. Roll-offs remain in the rental delivery/exchange/pickup workflow. Loading rental equipment onto the wrong assigned truck is refused. Collected loaded equipment cannot bypass its verified-disposal hold via empty-stock movement.

An imported deployed cart may have a known address but no original installation record. An owner/manager can physically verify it at its existing address and record a **legacy observation**. This preserves today's observation and the unknown original installation date. It cannot change the recorded address, reinterpret a roll-off rental or overwrite an existing active deployment. Subsequent recovery uses that verified observation. Observation type and deployment identity are immutable.

Every new movement/observation retains before/after snapshots, actor, reason and observed number. Rental completion also contributes delivery/collection evidence to this history. The office loads 50 events at a time using exclusive timestamp/ID cursors, with an older-events control and no repeated events across timestamp ties. The latest 50 placement records are shown; earlier placements remain in movement evidence. Original rental proof remains separately retained. Import rollback cannot retire equipment with subsequent physical activity.

Physical stocktake and installed-device/yard acceptance remain required. A verified disposal currently returns a roll-off to unspecified yard stock; the office must record its actual yard/truck destination. No yard or carrier is guessed from the dumping ticket.
