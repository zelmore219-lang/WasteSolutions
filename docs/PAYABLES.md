# Vendor bills and recorded settlement

Finance roles can record an already paid receipt or an unpaid vendor bill from Expenses. An unpaid bill needs a due date on or after its expense date. Recording the bill debits its expense category and credits accounts_payable; recording a partial/final payment debits accounts_payable and credits cash. Payments do not recognize the expense a second time. Journal posting dates use the bill/payment dates, and trial balance filtering honors posting dates.

Cash, check and bank-transfer settlements record money already paid. This feature does not initiate bank transfers, print/send checks or call a payment provider. References are unique and retained with the recorded method/date/actor. Bills and settlements are append-only. Concurrent payments serialize on the bill and cannot pay more than its remaining balance. Exact command retries return the original receipt without another posting; changing a retry payload is rejected.

Review bill shows the original amount, current unpaid amount and immutable payment history. The accounts_payable report reconstructs unpaid bills **as of the Through date**, including bills before the From date and excluding settlements after the Through date. Aging is based on that date and the bill due date, not today's date. CSV/PDF output is available through Reports. The receivables report also reconstructs invoice balances from dated journal postings and ages them as of its Through date. Later receipts, refunds and adjustments leave the earlier snapshot intact, including invoices now marked paid. The customer_balances report now reconstructs posted receivables and unused account-credit liability as of its Through date, including provider-credit receipts, credit transfers, applications and refunds. Later activity does not alter earlier snapshots; account names reflect the current customer.

Legacy payable rows, if any, migrate with a due date equal to their original expense date; legacy payment dates use the existing recorded timestamp date. New payment dates must fall between the bill's date and today in the company timezone. Existing already paid receipts remain paid with zero open liability.

Finance can record a dated vendor credit up to a bill's unpaid balance. It debits accounts_payable and reverses the original expense category; it does not create a vendor cash refund or credit beyond an unpaid bill. Returned/voided bill payments can be reversed once with a date/reference/reason, debiting cash and reopening accounts_payable. Neither action moves bank money. Original bills/payments remain intact with immutable credit/reversal history. Concurrent payments and credits lock the same bill. Every dated cumulative bill balance is checked, so a backdated new payment cannot spend capacity reopened by a later reversal. Historical payable and expense reports honor both credit and reversal dates.

Vendor master records, vendor cash refunds, bank reconciliation/feeds, approvals, multi-bill payment grouping and native QuickBooks vendor/bill integration remain open. QuickBooks journal export does not support an accounts_payable mapping without the required vendor-entity integration; it must not silently export these as ordinary expenses or invent vendors.
