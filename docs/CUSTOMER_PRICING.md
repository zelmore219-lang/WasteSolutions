# Customer-specific service pricing

Customer pricing stores reusable, explicitly selected per-service rates for an account. A rate has a name, service kind, customer-facing terms, integer USD price, inclusive effective dates, optional service-location restriction, active state and version. Owners, managers and accountants maintain rates; office/dispatch staff can view them and create quoted work. Drivers and customers cannot browse these internal rate cards. Existing manual agreed-price order creation remains available.

In **Customer pricing**, select the account, save its rate, and choose **Create order at this rate**. The server verifies the account, active location, service kind, rate version, effective service date, active state and exact quoted amount in the same order transaction. A forged amount, cross-account/location selection or stale quote fails without creating work. Overlapping rate cards require an explicit choice; the application does not silently guess a preferred price. Refresh rates closes unsaved quote/edit forms before loading current versions.

The order retains the selected rate ID and a copy of its version, name, terms, kind, amount and effective dates. Database constraints bind the rate to the same customer and the snapshot amount/kind to the order. Later rate edits/deactivation do not alter existing orders or invoices. Rescheduling an already agreed order retains its agreed price; it does not automatically quote the new date. Rate edits require a reason and a current version, and are audited with before/after values.

Completed ordinary service invoices include a line using the saved quote name/terms. Verified zero-price services produce a settled zero-dollar invoice and a zero-dollar line; they do not create a zero-value journal posting, payment or cash receipt. Ordinary positive invoices still recognize receivables/revenue once. Service completion authorization, assignment and required tasks remain in force before invoicing.

This initial rate card covers individual ordinary service orders. Monthly agreement prices and roll-off rental/weight/extension packages use their existing separate workflows. Automatic recurring-contract pricebook selection, catalog units/container-size tiers, taxes, fuel/handling policies, negotiated amendments and import/export of rate cards remain open. A saved price card is not a public purchase/booking authorization.
