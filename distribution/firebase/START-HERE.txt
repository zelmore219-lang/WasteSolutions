COREWASTE — FIREBASE WINDOWS CLIENT 0.3.0 (x64)

This client uses your hosted CoreWaste system. Docker Desktop is NOT required.

1. Extract the ZIP into a writable folder.
2. Open CoreWaste-Firebase-0.3.0-x64.exe.
3. The Firebase address is already filled in:
   https://wastesoloutions.web.app/office
4. Choose Connect AFTER the cloud services have been activated.
5. Sign in with your verified, provisioned employee account. Administrators need MFA.
